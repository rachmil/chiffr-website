How it Works.

Follow these steps to get started with Chiffr:

  1. Download the app and sign up with your email address. You will receive a unique username, such as "strongtrumpet281".
  2. Create a new AES Key or Stream Cipher in the Encryption tab.
  3. In the Encryption tab, select the key you generated, and have your contact tap the download button on their device to receive it.
  4. Once the secret key is exchanged, an encrypted conversation will start automatically, ensuring all messages exchanged are secure.
  5. You can repeat Steps 2 and 3 to generate and share a new key whenever you like. This will not erase your chat history.

Frequently Asked Questions

Chiffr is a secure, private communication tool that provides quantum-resistant encryption by generating Stream Ciphers and AES keys locally and exchanging them securely via Bluetooth.

Our encrypted chat app allows users to choose between AES (Advanced Encryption Standard) or a stream cipher based on a pseudo-random number generator (PRNG) for encrypting messages. Encryption keys and ciphers are never stored off-device, ensuring they remain secure.

For key exchange, we use Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) Secure Connections, which employs Diffie-Hellman key exchange for encryption. Since this exchange occurs locally over BLE, it is not exposed to network traffic, reducing the risk of interception compared to internet-based key exchanges.

Both the AES key and the stream cipher are securely transmitted over BLE.

When generating the stream cipher, we offer the option to introduce entropy from images captured on the device. We acknowledge that the ChaCha CSPRNG is pseudo-random and not truly unpredictable. While PRNGs have theoretical vulnerabilities, they are widely used in industry, including banking applications, and are considered sufficiently secure.

We are transparent about these trade-offs and continuously work to improve security.

Quantum computing is advancing at an exponential rate, with the number of qubits increasing significantly year over year. Key exchange methods (e.g., Diffie-Hellman and Elliptical Curve Cryptography) rely on the difficulty of solving mathematical problems to securely establish a shared secret. Quantum computers could intercept and calculate these keys, enabling them to decrypt all communication between parties.

Chiffr facilitates key exchange over Bluetooth and the keys are never stored remotely. Both the Stream Cipher and AES-256 offer advanced quantum resistance.

Stream Ciphers are fundamentally resistant to quantum attacks. Unlike traditional asymmetric encryption methods, which rely on mathematical problems vulnerable to Shor’s algorithm, Chiffr's encryption ensures that even with large-scale quantum computers, brute-forcing the key remains impossible.

For AES-256, it's estimated that 295 million logical qubits are required to crack the algorithm, a milestone that quantum computers will not approach for many decades.

All data is transmitted via BLE using Secure Connections mode, incorporating LE Legacy Pairing with Secure Connections Pairing to prevent passive eavesdropping and MITM attacks. You will be asked to confirm a 6 digit code during your first transfer of a secret key with a contact.

A Stream Cipher is a binary data file generated from a psuedo random number generator (PRNG). When you share a Stream Cipher with a contact, each message will use a unique portion of the cipher for encryption, and it is never re-used for another message.

Exchanging a Stream Cipher or AES Key ensures both parties share a secure key for encrypting and decrypting messages. By avoiding internet-based transmission, the key remains private.

Each bit of the cipher is used exactly once for each bit of plaintext message and never re-used. Our Steam Cipher is 200-KB, which will last for approximately 5000 messages. Once exhausted, simply share a new Stream Cipher.
